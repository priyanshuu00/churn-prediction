# User Personas

## Primary Persona: Retention / Customer Success Manager

### Profile
- **Name:** Sarah
- **Role:** Retention Manager / Customer Success Manager (CSM)
- **Industry:** Telecommunications / SaaS
- **Technical Literacy:** Moderate. Comfortable with dashboards (Tableau/Excel) and CRM systems, but not a data scientist. Does not write SQL or Python.

### Responsibilities
- Monitoring the health of the customer base.
- Designing and executing retention campaigns.
- Reaching out to high-risk VIP accounts to prevent churn.
- Reporting to executive leadership on churn rates and Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) retention.

### Core Goals
- **Reduce Churn:** Keep the monthly churn rate below the target threshold.
- **Protect Revenue:** Identify and save high-value accounts (high MRR) before they cancel.
- **Maximize Efficiency:** Focus her limited time and budget on customers who are *actually* saveable, rather than blindly offering discounts to everyone.

### Frustrations & Pain Points
- **Reactive Firefighting:** Usually only finds out a customer is unhappy when they hit the "cancel" button.
- **"Black Box" Scores:** Sometimes given "churn risk scores" by the data team but doesn't understand *why* the score is high, making it hard to know how to fix it.
- **Generic Interventions:** Currently uses a one-size-fits-all approach (e.g., sending a generic 10% discount email) because she lacks personalized insights for each customer.
- **Data Silos:** Struggles to connect support ticket history (technical issues) with billing data (contract type) to see the full picture.

### How This Product Helps Sarah
Sarah will use the **AI-Powered Customer Churn & Retention Intelligence** dashboard to:
1. Instantly see a prioritized list of high-risk customers sorted by Revenue at Risk.
2. See exactly *why* a customer is at risk (e.g., "3 Tech Support tickets in the last month + Month-to-Month contract").
3. Receive an AI-generated Next-Best-Action (e.g., "Offer a free 1-month tech support upgrade and a 10% discount to switch to an annual contract").
