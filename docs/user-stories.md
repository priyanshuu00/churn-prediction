# User Stories

As a **Retention / Customer Success Manager**, I want to...

## Viewing Churn KPIs
- **View overall KPIs** on a centralized dashboard so that I can quickly assess the current health of the business (Churn Rate, Total Customers, Monthly Revenue).
- **Track historic churn trends** so that I can see if our retention efforts are improving over time.

## Identifying High-Risk Customers
- **See a prioritized list of at-risk customers** so that I know who to contact first.
- **Sort at-risk customers by Revenue at Risk** so that I can prioritize high-value accounts that impact the bottom line the most.

## Understanding Churn Drivers
- **View the macro-level drivers of churn** (e.g., contract types, payment methods) so that I can recommend systemic changes to the product or billing teams.
- **See the specific reasons why an individual customer is flagged as high-risk** (e.g., frequent tech tickets, high monthly charges) so that I can tailor my conversation with them.

## Segmenting Customers
- **Filter the dashboard by customer demographics and service usage** (e.g., Fiber Optic users vs. DSL users) so that I can run targeted retention campaigns for specific cohorts.
- **Identify safe vs. high-risk segments** so that I don't waste retention budgets (discounts) on customers who are not going to leave.

## Receiving Retention Recommendations
- **Receive an AI-generated Next-Best-Action for high-risk customers** so that I don't have to guess what offer or intervention will save them.
- **Read clear, human-readable explanations** generated by the AI summarizing the customer's situation so that I can quickly get up to speed before calling them.

## Measuring Potential Business Impact
- **View the total Estimated Revenue Protected** so that I can quantify the ROI of my team's retention efforts.
- **Track the Retention Conversion Rate** (the percentage of high-risk customers we successfully saved) so that I can evaluate the effectiveness of the AI recommendations.
